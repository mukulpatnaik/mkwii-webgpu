Wii theme ideas

Three directions, all built on the same two reference frames: the Wii Menu (grey pinstriped ground, white rounded channel tiles, the curved bottom bar with the clock, light-blue accents) and the channel start screen (white, one big line of rounded text, a grey bar with the Wii Menu and Start pills). The toggle at the top flips every mock to the page's current navy-and-yellow, which becomes dark mode: one layout, two skins. The mocks are clickable: tiles open their channel screen, Wii Menu goes back.

A · The channel grid the Wii Menu itself as the menu

The four choices become channels. The Disc Channel is the game: it shows the loading bar and its screen holds Start. Play with friends, Controls and Saves are channels of their own; each opens the white start screen with its content and the Wii Menu / Start bar from the second reference. The round Wii button is settings (theme, change disc), the Mail button is Problems (save a log and send it, with a badge when the page has something to report). The clock is real. Empty slots are deliberate: that is what the Wii Menu looks like.

A2 · The compact grid no empty slots, the disc as the main tile

Four columns, two rows, nothing empty. The Disc Channel takes a double-size block on the left, with a bigger disc and the loading bar across its bottom, and the four small channels sit beside it: Play with friends, Controls, Saves and Problems. With Problems as a tile, the Mail button becomes the room: it carries a badge when an invite link brought you here or friends are in your room. No page arrow, since there is only one page.

B · Same layout, Wii Menu skin the cheapest path: a token swap

Nothing moves. The header, the four-choice column, the panel and the footer stay where they are; only the colours, the ground, the tile borders and the footer's shape change. The selected choice gets the light-blue glow instead of the yellow ring, the footer becomes the curved bar with the loading line in Wii blue, and the panel's heading takes the big rounded type of the start screen. Start here is a Wii-blue glossy pill; the tokens sheet below shows the white and yellow options.

  • What changes. About sixty lines of CSS: the body.pregame #overlay variable block and the disc, nav, panel and footer rules. No markup, no script.
  • Before the disc. Same as today: the disc card alone, no column of choices. It is a white tile on the grey ground, with the dashed slot in Wii blue and the big rounded heading.

D · Two tiles B before the disc, then Play solo or Play with friends, tools in the bar

Before the disc this is B's page: the disc card alone under the header. Once the disc is accepted the card gives way to two big channel tiles, Play solo and Play with friends, with kart logos that grow a little under the pointer. The curved bar carries three round buttons: Saves on the left, Controls in the middle with a Wii Remote icon, Help on the right. No clock or date; the game's loading line sits in the bay above Controls. The header keeps the title, the disc chip and Change disc. Play solo opens the Ready to race screen with Start; the round buttons open their screens the same way.
